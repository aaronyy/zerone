Selected case study · Vietnam market entry

European Wellness.
Trust had to come first.

The challenge

New market. Complex healthcare. The proposition was still unfamiliar locally, especially in a sensitive conversation involving children and families.

The strategic response

“Vì Sức Khoẻ Tinh Thần Trẻ Em Việt Nam”: a locally relevant awareness platform built around education, expert voices and trust, rather than leading with the treatment.

Outcome

500+ families reached nationwide.
3 earned media features.
Awareness → engagement → revenue. Commercial conversion exceeded expectations.

Contribution

Strategic and project director. Clarified the challenge and helped shape a brand and engagement direction that connected the project with the people it needed to reach.

11 / Selected case study · Vietnam market entry

European Wellness. Trust had to come first.

Strategic and project director. A healthcare project with no real precedent.

The challenge

New market. Complex healthcare. Trust had to come first. The proposition was still unfamiliar locally, especially in a sensitive conversation involving children and families.

The team was struggling to engage and reach the intended patient audience.

A healthcare business entering Vietnam needed a reason to be noticed that was not the treatment itself.

The strategic response

“Vì Sức Khoẻ Tinh Thần Trẻ Em Việt Nam”

A locally relevant awareness platform built around education, expert voices and trust, rather than leading with the treatment. Clarified the challenge and helped shape a brand and engagement direction that connected the project with the people it needed to reach.

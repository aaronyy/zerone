Founder’s point of view

How do patients judge quality before they experience your care?

Most doctors and healthcare founders I meet find this question difficult to answer. They want the right patients to know them, understand what they do well and choose them or their clinic.

But patients today can choose from many qualified doctors and similar clinics. Clinical expertise alone may not make it clear why one doctor or clinic is different.

I believe that difference must first be clearly defined, then translated into what patients see and experience. That is where Zerone works: helping doctors and healthcare founders define that difference and express it through a clear brand and patient experience, so patients can understand it, trust it and choose with confidence.

Thao Nguyen Tran, Founder
